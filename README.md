## 🕹️ About

WARDOGS is a large-scale tactical first-person shooter developed by BULKHEAD and published by Team17. Set in the derelict industrial mountains of Eastern Europe, it features up to 100 players split across three teams fighting for control of dynamic zones. The game combines tactical gunplay, vehicles, construction, destruction, and a persistent cash-driven loadout system. WARDOGS enters Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026.

WARDOGS is for players who enjoy teamwork-focused military shooters, large-scale PvP battles, tactical decision-making, and destructible environments.

## ✨ Features

- 🔫 **Tactical All-Out Warfare** — Engage in grounded first-person combat across a large-scale military sandbox.
- 👥 **100-Player Battles** — Fight alongside and against up to 100 players across three competing teams.
- 🎯 **Control Zone Warfare** — Capture randomized control zones in a King of the Hill-inspired team mode.
- 🚙 **Combined Arms Combat** — Coordinate infantry and armored vehicles to control the battlefield.
- 🏗️ **Build and Destroy** — Fortify key positions or demolish structures to create tactical opportunities.
- 💵 **Persistent Cash Economy** — Earn money through combat and teamwork, then spend it on equipment and loadouts.
- 🗣️ **Local Voice Chat** — Communicate with nearby teammates and enemies through proximity-based voice chat.
